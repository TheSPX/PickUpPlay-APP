How it works

From “anyone free?” to kick-off in three taps

1

Tell us how you play

A 30-second evaluation per sport — experience, how often you play, your level and your position — sets your skill tier.

2

Find or host a game

Browse games near you filtered by sport and level, or create one: pick the court, time, skill range and spots.

3

Show up and play

Chat with your group, check the forecast, split fair teams — then rate teammates so levels stay honest.

Features

Everything a pickup game needs

Built around one idea: every game should be fun for everyone on the court.

Skill matching

Beginner, Intermediate or Competitive — games show their range so you never end up outmatched.

Game chat

Every game has its own thread for the group — who's bringing the ball, running late, or bringing a friend.

Fair team split

One tap balances two sides by skill score and position — one keeper per side when possible.

Peer ratings

After a game, teammates rate whether your level was accurate. Five matching votes move it up or down.

Waitlist

Game full? Join the queue — when a spot opens, the next player in line is added automatically.

Live weather

See the expected temperature and chance of rain at the court for the exact hour your game starts.

Daily Quests

Level up off the court, too

Every day brings a new set of quests that grow with your rank. Clear them all to keep your streak alive.

  • Steps counted automatically from your phone
  • Push-ups, sit-ups, squats and a daily drill for your sport
  • Bonus XP for joining and hosting games
  • Climb from E-Rank to S-Rank

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is PickupPlay free?

Yes. Finding, joining and hosting games is free.

How is my skill level decided?

When you sign up you answer a few quick questions per sport. Your answers give a 0–100 score that sets your tier, and you can nudge it by one level. After games, teammates' ratings keep it accurate.

Where can I play?

PickupPlay currently covers the NJUPT campus courts — football fields, basketball and tennis courts, multi-sport grounds and the billiards room.

What if a game is full?

Join the waitlist. If a player leaves, the first person in line takes the spot automatically.

Can I use it in dark mode?

Yes — choose Light, Dark or the PickUp theme from your profile.
