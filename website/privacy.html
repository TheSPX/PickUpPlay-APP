Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

What we collect

  • Account: your email, display name and optional profile photo.
  • Sports profile: the sports you play and your evaluation answers (experience, frequency, level, position) and resulting skill tier.
  • Games: games you host or join, chat messages you send in game chats, and ratings you give teammates.
  • Daily Quests: quest progress, XP and streaks. Step counts are read on your device to update quest progress.
  • Location: used on your device to show nearby games and the campus map. We don't store a history of your location.

How we use it

  • To match you with games at your skill level and show them to other players.
  • To run game chats, waitlists, team splits and peer ratings.
  • To calculate XP, ranks and streaks.

Who can see what

  • Other players can see your display name, photo, skill level and role.
  • Game chats are visible only to players in that game.
  • Ratings are anonymous — players can't see who rated them.
  • Your XP history and quest progress are private to you.

Third parties

Data is stored with Supabase. Weather forecasts are requested from Open-Meteo using the court's location only. We don't sell your data.

Your choices

You can change your name and photo in your profile at any time. To delete your account and data, email hello@pickupplay.app.
